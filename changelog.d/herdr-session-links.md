### Added
- A private link opens one herdr session for whoever you give it to: Ovis shows and serves only that
  session, with no Settings, devices or updates, and the terminals run as the PC's account. Make one from the PC's or a session's Manage menu
  (**Private link…**), copy it when it is shown (it is shown once, and the PC stores only a hash of its
  token), and revoke it any time to end its access and close its connections at once.
